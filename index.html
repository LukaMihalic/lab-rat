Import a plate layout

Paste the absorbances from the plate reader into the grid (click A1, Ctrl+V; an export with A–H / 1–12 headers lands in place wherever you paste). Each named well's value goes to that standard or sample in the tables, in well order as replicates.

Click a cell and paste from Excel (Ctrl+V): the cells fill from there, like in Excel. Wells with the same name get the same colour.

Ct values

Paste three columns from the instrument export: sample, gene, Ct. "Undetermined" is fine. A header row is skipped.

ΔΔCt and fold change

By group

ΔCt = Ct(gene) − mean Ct(reference genes); ΔΔCt = ΔCt − mean ΔCt of the control group; fold = 2−ΔΔCt (assumes ~100% primer efficiency).

Protein concentrations

Measurements

Measure the bands in Fiji, copy the Results table and paste it under the antibody. Values go to the wells of the gel in order; the ladder is skipped.

Samples and groups

Groups are guessed from sample names (C-1, C-2 → C). Change them here; they apply to every gel.

Results

Each sample: antibody ÷ its normalizer, then ÷ the mean of the control group on the same membrane (control = 1).

Fiji macro
  1. Open the blot in Fiji, ideally the raw 16-bit TIFF (not a JPG).
  2. Draw one rectangle over all lanes: left edge of the first lane to the right edge of the last, a little above and below the bands.
  3. Plugins → Macros → Run… and pick the macro file, or paste it into Plugins → New → Macro and press Run.
  4. Enter the number of lanes. The values are copied; paste them here under the antibody.
